# Pool Strip Reader

Photograph an HTH Early Warning 4-way test strip with your phone and get an exact, ordered dosing plan
for your pool. Publishes pool sensors and reminders to Home Assistant, and can serve a
password-protected HTTPS address for phones away from the sidebar.

An optional 3D-printed light box gives steadier readings; its files are in the
[repository](https://github.com/DuncanRae/pool-strip-reader/tree/main/enclosure).
